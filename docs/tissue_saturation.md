## When each tissue fills fastest

The reading is the step at which a tissue's saturation rises most, under the registered sustained
recurrence. Two other readings were computed and are not used: the saturation *level* under that
recurrence rises monotonically to a fixed point, so it has no peak, and an impulse with a
mean-aggregating adjacency contracts from the first step, so 67 of 68 tissues peak at step 0 under it. Both are in
`data/processed/tissue_saturation/curves.csv` for anyone who wants them.

- peak step: min 1, median 3, max 20, over 15 distinct steps
- **tissues that fill fastest within the registered 8 steps: 42 of 68**, so 26 are cut off before their fastest step
- median pairwise correlation between the normalised curves: 0.183 (quartiles -0.669 and 0.795), so the curves separate rather than tracking one another
