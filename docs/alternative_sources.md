## Caveats recorded by the fetch

- (1) The Open Targets bucket is requester-pays (HTTP 400 UserProjectMissing on both the JSON listing API and direct object GETs); gcloud has no credentialed account and no GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS, so none of molecule, mechanismOfAction, indication, targets, interaction, baselineExpression, FAERS or disease parquet could be obtained; the measurements requested for those (action types, interaction signs, MedDRA level of FAERS, yearOfFirstApproval, cross-references) are therefore unmeasured. Providing a GCP project id with billing (gsutil -u PROJECT) would unblock it. The Open Targets licence could not be fetched (platform.opentargets.org is outside the allowed hosts); the platform states its data are CC0 1.0, unverified here. (2) Substitutes are older or differently scoped: DrugCentral snapshot is 2017 and DrugBank parse 2016-2019 (DrugBank is CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial), SIDER 4.1 is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with licensed MedDRA terms (do not redistribute term tables), ATC index is CC BY-NC-SA with WHO copyright. (3) cosmosR/PHONEMeS PKNs are OmniPath-derived aggregates without per-edge source database or references; the sibling-agent file data/raw/omnipath/omnipath_interactions.tsv

## What is kept for version 1

- OnSIDES v3.1.1 [29] (CC BY 4.0) as the later label time slice of evidence class E2: 6,928,666 label-event mentions over 41,119 labels and 1,866 ingredients; the measured name bridge maps 861 ingredients to 909 SIDER PubChem identifiers, to be replaced by a UniChem bridge now that ebi.ac.uk is reachable. OnSIDES carries no label dates, so the later slice is dated by its release, not per label.
- GTEx v10 median gene TPM [31] (59,033 genes, 13 brain tissues) for the brain expression weight on gene nodes (design 4.1) and the transcription-factor restriction (3.3).
- EFO v3.94.0 for the nervous-system indication proxy (MONDO:0005071 closure, 4,413 terms) and the Open Targets target-safety curation (Apache 2.0) as a candidate soft prior.
- DoRothEA [30] stays as a comparison regulon set only; CollecTRI from OmniPath is the transcription layer.
- DrugBank-derived tables are excluded on license grounds (CC BY-NC).
