Generated by `experiments/run_comparative_checks.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Checks: `configs/comparative_checks.csv` (214). Graph: `data/processed/graph_full_neuronal_split_binders_drugs_v4_enzymes`, drugs seeded on their own nodes. Table: `data/processed/evidence_full_v4`.

A finished run is asked the development checks only: its data have the lockbox removed.
