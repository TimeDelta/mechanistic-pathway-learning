# Adversarial review of design version 0.4: confirmed findings and dispositions

Produced on 3 October 2026 by a six-lens review (HPO semantics and curation, statistics, model and optimization, code correctness, reproducibility and documentation, scientific premises) with up to three independent verifiers per finding. Of 73 findings, 11 were confirmed before the verifier budget ran out; the 62 unverified findings are listed in the workflow transcript and are not treated as established. Dispositions below are the orchestrator's.

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
