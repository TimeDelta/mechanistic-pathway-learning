# Adversarial review of design version 0.4: confirmed findings and dispositions

Produced on 3 October 2026 by a six-lens review (HPO semantics and curation, statistics, model and optimization, code correctness, reproducibility and documentation, scientific premises) with up to three independent verifiers per finding. Of 73 findings, 11 were confirmed before the verifier budget ran out; the 62 unverified findings are listed in the workflow transcript and are not treated as established. Dispositions below are the orchestrator's.

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | blocking (statistics) | Under the subsystem and curated-module hold-outs every held-out set has 3 to 19 perturbations, so no per-set macro metric exists and the pooled metric carries the base-rate artifact of section 6.2. Verifiers reproduced the hold-out sizes and the artifact; they also showed that the reviewer's rank/n normalisation injects a hold-out-size signal (a constant predictor scores 0.282 under it) and that tie-neutral normalisations (rank/(n+1), z-scores, within-hold-out centring, stratified AUROC) keep popularity lowest while shrinking the gap between popularity and the graph baselines from about 0.11 to 0.02 to 0.04. The raw scores of degree-scaled popularity, the random walk and TransE carry almost no base-rate component (correlation with test base rate -0.04 to +0.05 against -0.885 for popularity). | Fixed (commit 89a3990): the harness pools rank-within-hold-out scores (rank over n + 1) and reports the stratified AUROC beside the raw metrics; section 7 defines the pathway-wise endpoint on the normalised macro AUPRC; the slice and full-data tables are being regenerated. |
| 2 | blocking (curation) | NOT-qualified annotations enter as grade-A positives because genes_to_phenotype.txt carries no qualifier (479 keys, 826 rows file-wide; CPT1C, GLRX5 and PDE10A on the slice), and the Excluded drop never fires (no HP:0040285 in the release). | Fixed in the report-level assembler: NOT rows are zero-valued reports; CPT1C becomes all-negative with weight 0 and no label; section 12 records the one-pair difference from the slice sweeps. |
| 3 | major (curation) | 0/N patient fractions were positives with frequency 0 (eight slice rows, all cognitive impairment). | Fixed: 0/N rows are absence reports; 12 full-graph pairs lost their positive label (section 12). |
| 4 | blocking (curation) | Grade A admits POLYGENIC OMIM associations (582 file-wide, 19 target rows on the slice) and every Orphanet association, which HPO types UNKNOWN; verifiers found Orphanet entries of type modifier, major susceptibility factor, candidate gene and role in the phenotype behind slice pairs. | Fixed (commit 6bb4f13): association types joined per report; grade A requires a disease-causing association; 608 full-graph gene pairs became grade C and left the label set (2,997 positives remain); the full-data runs were restarted on the corrected table. |
| 5 | major (curation) | The frequency weight clip(4f, 0.25, 1) ignores the patient count (1/1 outweighs a cohort-level Occasional) and the breakpoints have no derivation; verifiers judged the severity overstated because the clip saturates at f >= 0.25. | Recorded as a pre-registered sensitivity arm: a Beta-shrunk frequency (prior at the class midpoint, strength 2) and the soft-target option of open question 8; the frequency and sample size already enter the reliability model as rubric features. |
| 6 | major (curation) | Under the provenance policy grade C is unreachable for monogenic records (861 of 861 are A). | Fixed with finding 4: grade C is reached by 609 full-graph pairs. |
| 7 | major (curation) | Time split: 17 of 144 post-cutoff positives are biocuration events on long-standing OMIM synopses, and a cited publication can predate the gene-disease discovery, so the publication date is not the availability date without qualification. | Partly fixed (commit 1185958): the date source is carried per report and per pair, and the harness reports the time split with and without curation-dated post-cutoff pairs; the gene-disease association date approximation remains open (section 6.1). |

Findings not reached by the verifiers (62) remain unassessed; the headings in the transcript include the permuted-label control training on unpermuted validation labels, the aggregator's handling of permuted runs, isozyme twins across folds, the description-length penalty against AdamW, hard-concrete expectation at evaluation, and the Slurm requeue logic for arrays. They are queued for the next review round.

## OnSIDES later-slice review (5 October 2026)

Produced by a three-lens review (code correctness, scientific validity, documentation and style) of the OnSIDES bridge, loader, builder, tests and pages, with one adversarial verifier per non-minor finding; fifteen findings were confirmed (four of them duplicates across lenses) and applied in the same workflow, then the full table was re-assembled (section 12 of the design). Numbers before the fixes refer to the 1,277-statement build.

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | blocking | "OnSIDES-only" pairs were measured against the qualified SIDER table, so 213 of the 371 were on 2015 SIDER labels of the same drug (178 through the unified identifier, 35 through a name-matched identifier whose ChEMBL mapping is empty). | Fixed: the overlap has two SIDER sides, the qualified SIDER-label reports of the assembled table (read by source) and the raw SIDER 4.1 PT events for every identifier linked to the perturbation. Current build: 587 OnSIDES pairs, 283 in both, 304 absent from the qualified table, of which 204 on 2015 labels and 100 later-slice candidates (41 on perturbations SIDER also holds, 59 on OnSIDES-only perturbations); design 6.1 defines the pharmacological time split on the candidates. |
| 2 | blocking | label_section NA is every non-US label (EU, UK and JP events; no US event), not an unsectioned mention; 681 of 1,277 qualifying reports carried the wrong evidence code, limitation text and section rubric value, and the section feature duplicated the country feature. | Fixed: NA maps to evidence code non_us_label_section, counts as adverse-reactions text for rubric_adverse_reactions_section, and the limitation clause reads "non-US label, section not recorded by OnSIDES"; the docstring, the page and the specification state that section codes exist for US labels only. |
| 3 | blocking | The ATC waiver admitted ingredients with no ATC code (30 ingredients, 121 reports, 75 of the 371 OnSIDES-only pairs, mostly peripherally acting: carbidopa, benserazide, epoetin alfa, sacubitril and others), a looser bar than the SIDER rule. | Fixed: no waiver; an ingredient without an ATC code fails with no_atc_code (130 statements); rubric_atc_known stays a feature. Qualifying reports 1,144 from 127 ingredients. |
| 4 | blocking | The RxClass filter kept only entries whose minConcept RxCUI equals the queried ingredient, dropping ATC codes RxNav attaches to precise-ingredient (PIN) salts; four non-nervous-system drugs qualified through the waiver and three lost their N code. Reported by two lenses. | Fixed: IN and PIN entries kept, MIN entries dropped, untyped entries kept only for the queried RxCUI; ingredients with an ATC code 1,472 to 1,523; test covers PIN, untyped and PIN-only cases. |
| 5 | blocking | Statements from combination-product labels were attributed to every ingredient (169 of 1,277 qualifying statements had no single-ingredient label behind them; carbidopa alone carried 30). | Fixed: single_ingredient_label_count is carried per statement; a statement whose every label is a combination product is disqualified with combination_label_only (96) and flagged in its limitations; loader test with a two-ingredient label. |
| 6 | should fix | The bridge fetch keyed mechanisms by molecule only while the builder keyed them by molecule and parent, so the bridge counts described a rule the builder did not apply (291 ingredients flipped) and 64 targets the builder used were never fetched (17 of the 28 no_graph_node disqualifications).
