# Data on a drug's concentration at its site of action: how many study drugs have any

Generated by `experiments/label_review/effect_site_coverage.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Why this table exists. A prediction at the peak of a drug's concentration at its site of action in the brain needs, per drug, the extent of unbound brain exposure (the unbound brain-to-plasma partition coefficient, Kp,uu), the rate at which the brain follows plasma, and for a check a measured target occupancy over time. This counts the study drugs for which each was found in a retrieved source.

How to read it. A drug is counted when a retrieved table names it; the count is of drugs, not of measurements, and many tables repeat one original value. The tables were copied by three research passes on 10 and 11 October 2026 from the sources listed beside them (`data/raw/label_review/effect_site_tables/*/sources.md`, not in git); a row was written only for a compound and value read in a retrieved file. Sources that could not be retrieved are listed there too, so every count is a lower bound on what is published and the keyword counts at the end are upper bounds. Most extent and rate values are from rats or mice.
