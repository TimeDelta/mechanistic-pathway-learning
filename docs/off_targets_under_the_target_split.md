## What requiring a measured occupancy costs

- drugs with a reading: **121** of 222; without: **101**
- kept positive drug pairs carried by drugs with a reading: **810** of 1076 (75.3%); the rest, **24.7%**, leaves with them

The missingness is not random. A drug needs a published therapeutic range and a fraction unbound, which newer drugs are least likely to have, so the drugs that leave skew modern and include a good deal of the pharmacology a psychiatric study is for:
