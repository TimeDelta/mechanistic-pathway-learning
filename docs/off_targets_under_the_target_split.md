# Off-targets under the item 25 split, and the cost of requiring a measured occupancy

Generated by `experiments/label_review/off_targets_under_the_target_split.py`.

> **This is a sensitivity reading of an alternative the registration declined, not an open question.** Preregistration item 25 splits the held-out set on **mechanism targets** and reports off-target sharing through its secondary stratum 25c; off-targets appear nowhere in item 25. The table below asks what admitting them to the split would have cost. Its "threshold" is therefore hypothetical: there is no registered threshold, and none is wanted, because all off-targets enter the **model input** at their occupancy (item 5c step 1), where an engagement near zero contributes near zero on its own. **Every row of that table is measured on the drugs the occupancy table covers, not on the study.** The occupancy table holds only the drugs with a measured concentration, so the mechanism-only row stands on 115 drugs and 75 targets where item 25's registered split stands on 222 drugs and 192 targets. The last column is the same quantity as item 25c's secondary stratum and the two numbers differ for that reason alone: **38.6 percent here against the 21.2 percent item 25c registers**, which is the full table. So this table prices the **shape** of the trade - admit weaker engagements as targets and a held-out drug shares more of them with training - and not the levels the study will see. The levels move at the v4 rebuild, which lifts item 1's three-target limit and so changes every drug's target set. A threshold chosen on these numbers should be chosen for the shape.

## What requiring a measured occupancy costs

- drugs with a reading: **121** of 222; without: **101**
- kept positive drug pairs carried by drugs with a reading: **810** of 1076 (75.3%); the rest, **24.7%**, leaves with them
