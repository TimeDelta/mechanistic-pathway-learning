# Open decisions

Every decision waiting on the owner, in one place, as of 12 October 2026. The registered subset of this list is also
in the "What is still open" block of `docs/preregistration.md`, which is canonical for anything registration touches;
this file adds the build decisions and the actions only the owner can take, which that block does not carry.

Nothing on this list is decided by a session. A session may measure an option and recommend, and several entries record
a recommendation and an objection, but the choice is the owner's.

**A decision is recorded here in the turn it is taken** (the owner, 13 October 2026: "You really need to start
recording decisions as they happen"). Five entries were still open on 13 October after the decision behind them had
been made and in some cases built - the multiplicity (5.1, decided 12 October), the probability clamp (4.5, decided
12 October), the Open Targets weight (1.4, decided that morning), and 4.1 and 4.3, whose text described a rule two
amendments out of date. Registering an amendment is not recording a decision: this file is where the owner reads
what is still his to settle, and an entry left open after the fact costs him a second ruling on a settled question.
