# Drug entry nodes: what they change before anything is trained

Generated by `experiments/measure_drug_entry_nodes.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Graph `data/processed/graph_full_neuronal_split_binders_drugs`, built from `data/processed/graph_full_neuronal_split_binders` by `experiments/build_drug_entry_node_variant.py`; evidence `data/processed/evidence_full_v3_parkinsonism`; label selection `data/processed/label_selection/better_v2_full_v3_parkinsonism.parquet`. Every encoder is at its random start (seed 0, 16 state channels, structural node features only), and two encoders that are compared share every parameter they have in common. No parameter is fitted and no outcome chooses anything.

## 1. What the build adds, and what the loader keeps

- drug nodes: **154**, one per drug perturbation; drugs attached to a graph compound instead: 0
- mechanism edges (`targets`): **791**, by sign -1.0: 338, -0.5: 19, 0.0: 46, 0.5: 309, 1.0: 79
- sequestration edges (`sequesters`, sign -1): **71** for 49 drugs, by carrier gene ALB: 39, ORM1: 16, ORM2: 16
- the graph: 49,728 nodes and 280,646 edges (nodes sha256 `f484ac76302773a8`, edges `b5b44fc4aa2281d0`)

Read with `drug_entry="targets"`, the ablation arm, the variant gives the source graph's arrays: of 40 fields of the loaded data, **0** differ, and the structural feature matrix is identical.

Read with `drug_entry="nodes"`, each of the 154 drug perturbations is seeded on its own node and the 1,414 gene perturbations keep their seeds. Identical to the source graph: perturbation_ids (yes), group_ids (yes), outcomes (yes), weights (yes), label_mask (yes), degrees for strata (yes). So the two arms share their folds, their label mask and their degree strata.

## 2. How many study drugs share a target

The 791 mechanism edges land on **129** target nodes, and **82** of those are the target of two or more study drugs (744 of the edges). The median target has 2 drugs and the most shared has 26: PROTEIN:GABRB1 (26), PROTEIN:GABRA1 (26), PROTEIN:GABRG2 (26), PROTEIN:GABRA2 (25), PROTEIN:GABRE (24).

This count is a property of which drugs the study contains. An encoder that averages a relation's messages over the edges entering a node divides each drug's signal at a shared target by it.

### The edge carrying the carrier's change, not its state

`--sequestration-carries change` (the trainer's default on a graph with the relation) sends along a sequestration edge how far the carrier has moved from its unperturbed state. In message passing, against the graph without the edges:

- drugs with a carrier whose own field changes: **0**; drugs without: 0 (largest absolute difference 3.6e-07).

The linear response is a deviation from rest, so it carries the change by construction. Each drug with a known carrier simulated alone, with its carriers lowered by one unit beside it, and the carriers lowered alone; the part that passes through the drug node is the second minus the first minus the third, and the factor is its size along the drug's own response, per channel. Nothing is trained: the sign of the factor is the graph's and its size is the untrained gains'.
